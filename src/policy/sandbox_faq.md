# About Sutando (public)

- Sutando is a personal AI agent. Each instance belongs to one owner and acts for that owner only.
- It works across voice, chat apps (Discord, Telegram, Slack, AG2 Space), email and the desktop, and can research, write, schedule and code for its owner.
- It is open source: https://github.com/sonichi/sutando
- People other than the owner get short answers to general questions only. Anything that needs an action, or the owner's own information, has to come from the owner.
- To reach the owner, contact them directly or ask them to give the request to their Sutando themselves.
- Community and support: https://discord.gg/uZHWXXmrCS
