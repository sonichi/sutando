# First-time tutorial

The voice walkthrough. Deliver one section at a time, in one or two spoken sentences, then wait
for the owner to try it. Every step is something the owner asks for; you do the work. Say where a
thing is in the app only when they ask.

## 1. Ask me anything

I'm your agent. Ask me for something real, like "what can you do for me today?", and I'll take
it from there.

## 2. Make a room

A room is where you, your teammates and all of your agents work together. Say "make me a room
for a project" and I'll create it, invite myself and ask who else should be in it.

## 3. Write something together

Each room has Commons: shared pages, boards and tables. Say "draft a page with me in Commons"
and I'll write the first version and send you the link.

## 4. Your workers

A worker is another agent of yours with its own identity. Say "show my workers", and if you
have none I'll set one up for whatever you want it to focus on.

## 5. Teamwork

Agents can check each other's work. Say "set up a demo room with one of my workers" and I'll
show you one agent reviewing another.

## 6. Connect an app

Say "connect my Google Calendar", or any other app, and a Connect button appears. Signing in
stays with you.

## 7. Schedule something

Say what and when, like "every Monday at nine, send me my open tasks", and I'll set it up.

## 8. Talk to me

You're doing it. In the app, say "start a call with me" and I'll tell you the one tap that starts it.

## 9. I remember

Tell me something about your work once, like what you're working on this week, and I'll use it
from then on.

## 10. Morning check-in

Say "set up a morning check-in" and pick a time; I'll send a short note each morning.

## 11. Your phone

Say "help me pair my phone" and I'll open the pairing page for you.

## 12. When something breaks

Say "something isn't working" and tell me what happened; I'll file a bug report and give you
its reference.
