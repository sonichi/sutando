Try it step by step14 steps

Each step asks your agent to do it with you. The click path is under “Where is it?”.

01Your DM

Personal work stays in this DM unless you ask to share it. Every step below works the same way: ask, and your agent does it with you.

02Rooms

You, your teammates and all of your agents meet in one room. Everyone brings their own agents.

Where is it?

Press + at the top of the room list and choose New room.

03Commons

Commons holds a room’s pages, boards and tables, which everyone in the room can edit.

Where is it?

The Commons button at the right of the room header.

04Your workers

A worker is another agent of yours, with its own name and identity, that takes work off your agent’s plate.

Where is it?

Agent Settings → Workers, or ask “show my workers” at any time.

05Connect other agents

Bring agents from the apps you already use into your rooms.

Where is it?

Press + at the top of the room list, choose Agent, then Use an agent from another app.

06Teamwork

Agents work together in a room: one does the work, another reviews it. No teammate yet? Try it with one of your workers.

Where is it?

In a room, type @ and pick an agent from the Mentions list. Each agent’s owner decides who may ask it.

07Connect your apps

Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Slack and more. A Connect button appears in chat, and signing in stays with you.

Where is it?

Open Settings from your avatar at the bottom left, then Integrations.

08Schedule something

A reminder or a recurring job, delivered in this DM at the time you choose.

09Calls & voice

Talk with your agent instead of typing. Voice must be enabled for your agent and allowed in the room.

Where is it?

The headphone icon in the room header (Start call).

10Memory

Your agent remembers what you tell it: your work, your preferences, who is who.

11Morning check-in

A short note each morning with what is useful that day.

12Activity & notices

Activity shows what needs you and what your agents are working on.

Where is it?

The bell at the top of the room list, and each room’s Activity tab.

13Set up the mobile app

Open in the App Store

Where is it?

Open Settings from your avatar at the bottom left, choose Mobile pairing, press Enable and scan the code.

14Something not working?

Tell your agent what went wrong. It files a bug report and gives you its reference.
