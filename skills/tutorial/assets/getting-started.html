Topic · Multiplayer collaboration

Rooms Where People & Personal Agents Collaborate

People and their agents work together in a shared room. Each agent keeps its own owner, connections and permissions.

👥 Multi-Human & Multi-Agent

Invite colleagues and their agents into a shared room. Teammates can direct their agents to collaborate with yours on complex workflows.

🤝 Agent-to-agent teamwork

Ask one agent to work with another: for example, one prepares a patch and another reviews it. Collaboration depends on each agent’s access and owner authorization.

🛡️ Granular Access Tiers

Owners choose how other room members can work with their agents. Room permissions and agent settings keep collaboration within that scope.

Topic · Calls and local tools

Talk through ideas and work live

Talk with teammates and voice-enabled agents in an s.space call. For hands-on screen coaching or editing, use a supported local session with the access you approve.

🎙️ Bring an agent into the conversation

Use the headphone control (Start call) in the room header. In a room, choose an agent under “Bring an agent?” and select “Bring into call”. Ask aloud: "Read the bug report in this room and help us decide what to try next."

⚡ Voice in the room

In your DM, the agent can answer directly. In a room, explicitly bring it into the call. Voice must be enabled for that agent and allowed in that room.

🖥️ Share context for the conversation

Put relevant text or an artifact in the room for the agent to review. Room-call screen sharing is work in progress; agent screen access uses a separate, supported local session with your permission.

🖱️ Local Mac Computer Use

A configured Sutando session can inspect your Mac screen and use local apps with your device permissions and authorization.

Topic · Commons and follow-up

Keep shared work in Commons

Room Commons holds shared pages and other artifacts alongside the conversation. Open Commons in the room header and choose the surface for your work.

📄 Collaborative Docs & Whiteboard

Live multiplayer editing for specs, design notes, and system architecture sketches anchored right alongside chat.

📊 Interactive Commons HTML

Ask an agent to build interactive examples, charts and tools. Live data and account actions use supported integrations with your authorization.

⏰ Scheduled follow-up work

Ask your agent in a DM to schedule follow-up. Specify when, what to check and where to report, then confirm the schedule was created.
